# Google Play listing

## Title

Political Strategy Simulator

## Short description

Master campaigns, coalitions, crises, and crowns in a deep strategy game.

## Category

Games · Strategy

## Positioning

Gain Power. Rule Nations. Shape History.

Political Strategy Simulator is a fictional, nonpartisan grand-strategy game. Run a 50-state presidential campaign, steer a fragile republic, secure a royal succession, or build a legitimate political transition. Every choice changes a persistent system of actors, factions, institutions, regions, resources, and delayed consequences.

All modes are free. Core gameplay works offline. No account, purchase, energy timer, or paid campaign is required.

## Important disclosure

All candidates, countries, polling, events, and outcomes are fictional simulations. The game is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government, election authority, candidate, or political party. It does not predict real elections.
